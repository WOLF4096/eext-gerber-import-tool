  `DELETE /releases/assets/{id}` → `POST https://uploads.github.com/repos/.../releases/{rid}/assets?name=<同名>`
  （`Content-Type: application/zip`，`--data-binary @file`）。**不要碰 release 本体**，body 就不会变。
